FUNDAMENTALS

Start with the essentials

01

Voltage

Electrical potential difference between two points, measured in volts. It provides the potential that drives current through a circuit.

02

Current

The flow of electric charge through a circuit, measured in amperes. Conventional current flows from higher to lower potential.

03

Resistance

The opposition to current flow, measured in ohms. Resistors are used to limit current and set voltage levels.

04

Ohm's Law

The relationship between voltage, current and resistance. It is one of the first tools used to analyse simple circuits.
