Common ECE calculations

OHM'S LAW

V = I × R

Know any two of voltage, current and resistance to find the third.

POWER

P = V × I

Electrical power can also be expressed as I²R or V²/R for a resistor.

RC

fc = 1/(2πRC)

Common cutoff-frequency relationship for a first-order RC filter.
